PROPAGATION GUIDE

How to read the numbers

Kp 0–2

Quiet geomagnetic conditions. Often favorable for stable HF paths and weak-signal work.

Kp 3–4

Unsettled to active conditions. Polar paths may become less reliable.

Kp 5+

Geomagnetic storm conditions. HF absorption and fading can increase, while auroral activity may improve.
