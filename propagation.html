Estimated HF Conditions

These ratings are a simple estimate from Kp and F10.7 flux. Time of day, season, path, antenna, local noise and solar events also matter.

How to Read It

Kp 0–2Quiet
Kp 3–4Unsettled
Kp 5+Storm

Higher solar flux often helps the upper HF bands. Higher Kp generally makes polar and higher-latitude paths less reliable.
