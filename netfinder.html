About this finder

HF nets are intentionally separated from local RF distance filtering because propagation matters more than mileage. RF distance is straight-line distance to the listed net/repeater area, not a coverage prediction. Terrain, antenna height, power and repeater coverage affect whether a signal is usable. AllStar and EchoLink entries are informational links only; KC8GW.com does not connect or control your radio or node.
